# और फिर — अधूरा शब्द (Aur Phir)

## Overview

The film's open hand: the [granth](../props/granth.md) ends unfinished, on half a word — "बहु", बहुरि, meaning "और फिर…". The practice is never completed on screen, and the child's "और फिर?" turns the gap into hope rather than loss.

## Expression

- अनाया's "और फिर?" after every story, explanation and scolding (P.1, 1.17, E.2).
- The half-written last page; "ending है — लिखी नहीं गई।" (7.4).
- The lamp that burns on after the pen is laid down (7.4, E.2).
- "और फिर… वो तू बताएगी।" — the hope handed to the youngest (E.2).
