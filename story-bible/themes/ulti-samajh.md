# उलटी समझ — एक रोग (Ulti Samajh)

## Overview

The story's claim: every fault in the house — restlessness, anger, pride, pretending, religion-as-insurance — is one disease, मिथ्यादर्शन / उलटी समझ: not taking a thing as it is. One illness, six faces. Nobody is cured; each person takes one small true step.

## Expression

- The [निदान बोर्ड](../props/nidan-board.md): every card threaded to one covered centre (1.16).
- The [चश्मा](../props/dadaji-ka-chashma.md) on the head — looking for what you already hold.
- The kitchen vaidya names the symptom from four daily wants (1.6).
- The centre card uncovered as मिथ्यादर्शन (2.8); then "बीमारी कपड़े बदलेगी" (4.5).
