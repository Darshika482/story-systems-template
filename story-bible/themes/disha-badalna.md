# दिशा बदलना — संक्रांति (Disha Badalna)

## Overview

The counter-claim and the cure: nothing new is needed, only a change of direction. संक्रांति is the sun turning course; the answer is already held, only faced the wrong way. The same want, "बस… दूसरी दिशा में."

## Expression

- The [चश्मा](../props/dadaji-ka-chashma.md) on the head (1.2 → 7.5) — the thing sought, already there.
- "बोना = … दूसरी दिशा में" — the discarded [cane](../props/ganne-ka-gamla.md) planted, not sucked (4.4).
- The missing ₹1 that was अर्जुन's own setting all along (7.1).
- मोक्षमार्ग glossed as "रास्ता दिखाने वाला दीया" (P.1).
