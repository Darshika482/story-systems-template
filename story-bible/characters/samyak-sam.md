# सम्यक् — "Sam" (Samyak)

## Overview

Class-10 student, 16; makes videos, fact-checks everything. Wants to be a person and not a tab in his father's sheet; needs to turn "prove it" on himself. His name is shouted across the flat as an ordinary word until दादाजी explains it (2.8).

## Traits

- "Logic?" / "Source?" — reverse-searches a forward in thirty seconds (6.1).
- Films first, asks later; the family is his raw material (1.10, 3.6).
- Recites Sanskrit shlokas perfectly — दादी taught him — and is mortified by it (2.4).
- Sleeps with a night light; says the true thing nobody else will.

## Relationships

- [दादाजी — हरिप्रसाद (Dadaji)](dadaji-hariprasad.md) — the first adult who enjoys his objections.
- [दादी — शांति देवी (Dadi)](dadi-shanti-devi.md) — home; she taught him every shloka.
- [अर्जुन (Arjun)](arjun.md) — a mirror neither can see.
- [अनाया (Anaya)](anaya.md) — protector, in secret.

## Arc

Eye-roller with a camera who learns to turn his sharpest tool on himself and fills his own stream-change form (7.2), then asks before he films (E.1).

## Knowledge

- Learns his own name's meaning (सम्यक् = "सही, जैसा है वैसा") only in 2.8.
