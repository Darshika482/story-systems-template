# अनाया (Anaya)

## Overview

Class-2 child, 7 (8 by E.1). She does not need fixing; she is the story's honest listener, asking the blunt question at the worst moment. Her "और फिर?" is the film's last line.

## Traits

- "और फिर?" at the end of every story, explanation and scolding (P.1, 1.17, E.2).
- Claims things — "मेरी पतंग।" (1.2); keeps her crayons in strict colour order.
- Short sentences, concrete nouns; she notices, she does not philosophise.
- Frightened of balloons bursting; talks to the तुलसी.

## Relationships

- [दादी — शांति देवी (Dadi)](dadi-shanti-devi.md) — her audience; she wakes in दादी's bed.
- [दादाजी — हरिप्रसाद (Dadaji)](dadaji-hariprasad.md) — answers her honestly, including "हाँ".
- [सम्यक् — "Sam" (Samyak)](samyak-sam.md) — secret protector who draws her projects.

## Arc

Claims a fallen kite in January; by the next January she lets one go — "ये रुकने नहीं आई थी।" (E.1). Her insight comes from noticing, never from wisdom beyond her age.

## Knowledge

- None tracked. She asks the death question "गुप्ता अंकल कहाँ गए?" (1.8) that nobody answers.
