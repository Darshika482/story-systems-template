# अर्जुन (Arjun)

## Overview

Delivery manager, 43; runs his family as a spreadsheet. Wants "सब सेट रहे"; needs to see that the restlessness begins inside him, not outside. First seen at 1:52 a.m. over a missing rupee (P.2).

## Traits

- Cannot let one rupee go; reconciles every bill — ₹2,841 vs ₹2,840 (P.2, 7.1).
- Plans other people's lives in cells and colours (1.11 spreadsheet).
- Recurring tells: "मैंने किया!" on success, "ऐसा ही होना था।" on failure (1.10).
- Terrified of lizards; cries at ads with old parents and blames the fan.

## Relationships

- [मीरा (Meera)](meera.md) — wife; she is right about him and he resents the timing.
- [दादाजी — हरिप्रसाद (Dadaji)](dadaji-hariprasad.md) — father; love expressed as accuracy.
- [सम्यक् — "Sam" (Samyak)](samyak-sam.md) — son; he repeats his own father word for word.
- [दादी — शांति देवी (Dadi)](dadi-shanti-devi.md) — mother; still her boy.

## Arc

Lie: "मैं कर लूँगा — मैं कर्ता हूँ"; truth: he is not the doer of what happens outside him. See [Arjun: fixer to searcher](../arcs/arjun-fixer-to-searcher.md). Carries the 1999 wound "ग्यारह कहाँ गए?".

## Knowledge

- Through 4.1–4.4 he is on voice rest and cannot speak; first words again at 4.5.
- Does not learn the missing ₹1 is his own 2024 auto-pay setting until 7.1.
