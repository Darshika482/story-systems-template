# गुप्ता अंकल (Gupta Uncle)

## Overview

रमेश गुप्ता, 74, of flat A-204; दादाजी's garden-bench partner since 1987. Never shown as a full figure — only a voice on the phone, then a garlanded photo. His sudden death is the film's inciting incident. He shared the postponement line "उम्र हो गई, अब धर्म-ध्यान करेंगे।"

## Traits

- Met as sound and image only: a phone voice (1.7), then a garlanded photo (1.8).
- Late to the bench for the first time since 1987 — the morning he dies (1.7).
- Owns half of the garden bench; his half stays empty after him (1.17, E.0).

## Relationships

- [दादाजी — हरिप्रसाद (Dadaji)](dadaji-hariprasad.md) — forty years of dawn walks and the same deferred vow.
- [अनाया (Anaya)](anaya.md) — sits, unknowing, on his half of the bench (1.17).

## Arc

A flat figure whose absence does the work: his death produces अर्जुन's one-night vow and the whole chain of the film.
