# दादी — शांति देवी (Dadi Shanti Devi)

## Overview

The grandmother, 68, and the film's narrator. Wants everyone safe, fed, home before dark; needs to see her religion has become insurance and that the book she heard through a door for fifty years was speaking to her. Opens the film telling अनाया a bedtime story (P.1).

## Traits

- Forwards every teaching to someone else — "ये बात ___ को सुनानी चाहिए।"
- Bargains with fate in numbers: fasts, माला, coins; keeps a fasts notebook since 1977 in numerals.
- Feeds people as her way of saying anything; answers "why" with "हमारे घर में होता आया है।"
- Cricket radio and mental arithmetic faster than her son's spreadsheet.

## Relationships

- [दादाजी — हरिप्रसाद (Dadaji)](dadaji-hariprasad.md) — fifty years of bickering; he has never thanked her.
- [मीरा (Meera)](meera.md) — rival and only real ally; they compete in sacrifice.
- [अर्जुन (Arjun)](arjun.md) — still her boy; she checks if he has eaten.
- [अनाया (Anaya)](anaya.md) — her audience.

## Arc

From amused guide to everyone's faults to the woman who reads at last. See [Dadi: narrator becomes reader](../arcs/dadi-narrator-becomes-reader.md) and [the fifty-year promise](../arcs/fifty-year-promise.md).

## Knowledge

- Knows the granth by ear the whole film and hides that she reads slowly ("मेरा चश्मा नहीं मिल रहा").
- The house learns she knows it by ear only in 6.6; before that only a lips-moving hint in 5.5.
