# मीरा (Meera)

## Overview

Former maths teacher, 40; now runs the flat and tutors society children. Wants everything correct and seen to be correct; needs to drop "मेरे बिना ये घर एक दिन नहीं चलेगा". Her one-liners to अर्जुन are the sharpest in the film.

## Traits

- A list for everything — "लिस्ट में था।" (the yellow fridge pad).
- Measures herself against Mrs Kapoor's status (3.2).
- Migraines since she stopped teaching — "बस ये दर्द चला जाए" (1.4).
- Gets the giggles at the worst moments; merciless at carrom.

## Relationships

- [अर्जुन (Arjun)](arjun.md) — husband she manages; misses being his friend.
- [दादी — शांति देवी (Dadi)](dadi-shanti-devi.md) — rival and only real ally.
- [सम्यक् — "Sam" (Samyak)](samyak-sam.md) — her project, which he resents.
- [अनाया (Anaya)](anaya.md) — the one person she lets win an argument.

## Arc

Lie: perfect practice without understanding is enough. Truth: she and दादी both thought they alone held the house up (6.7). Ends by sending her school application back, a blank line on her list.

## Knowledge

- In 5.5 she is the only one who sees दादी's lips move with the reading — she knows दादी can follow the granth before the house does (reveal 6.6).
