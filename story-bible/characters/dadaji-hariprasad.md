# दादाजी — हरिप्रसाद (Dadaji Hariprasad)

## Overview

Retired head cashier (State Bank of Bikaner & Jaipur, 1977–2014), 72; reads the granth at dawn. The kitchen vaidya who diagnoses the family's one illness. Wants to hand the book to his son before cataract blinds both eyes; needs to see his own case among the others.

## Traits

- Glasses on his head while he hunts for them (1.2, 3.2, 7.5).
- Says less than he knows; answers a question with a smaller question, and "हम्म।"
- Checks everything like a cashier holds a note to the light — thirty-seven years of counting.
- Punctual to the minute; frightened of doctors and needles, and hides it.

## Relationships

- [दादी — शांति देवी (Dadi)](dadi-shanti-devi.md) — the one he most needs to thank and never has.
- [अर्जुन (Arjun)](arjun.md) — his son; love expressed entirely as accuracy.
- [सम्यक् — "Sam" (Samyak)](samyak-sam.md) — delighted by the boy's objections.
- [अनाया (Anaya)](anaya.md) — answers her honestly, including "हाँ".

## Arc

Amused vaidya who becomes a frightened patient and a co-learner read to by his son. See [गिनती: ग्यारह कहाँ गए](../arcs/gyarah-kahan-gaye.md) and [the fifty-year promise](../arcs/fifty-year-promise.md).

## Knowledge

- Pins his own case face-down on the निदान बोर्ड (1.16); content revealed only in 6.8 — "ग्यारह कहाँ गए?", "ये रोग मैंने दिया तुझे".
- Carries guilt over the 1999 sentence to his son throughout.
