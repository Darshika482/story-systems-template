# फ़्लैट B-502 (Flat 502)

## Overview

B-502, fifth floor, सूर्योदय रेज़िडेंसी, मानसरोवर, जयपुर; nameplate "हरिप्रसाद जैन". Home to the three-generation family and the stage for almost the entire film.

## Geography

- Fifth floor, reached by lift or the stairwell (the lift fails in 7.3).
- Rooms: master bedroom, children's room, grandparents' room, kitchen, dining, living room, balcony.
- दादाजी's room holds the निदान बोर्ड and a stopped clock at 4:20.
- The डॉलहाउस section view shows three rooms across at once (1.1, E.2).

## Sensory Details

- At 2 a.m. the kitchen glows gold (reading) while the bedrooms glow blue (phone screens).
- "तीन बत्तियाँ" — count the lights to find who is awake.
- Monsoon seepage stains three walls from one cracked pipe (5.2).
