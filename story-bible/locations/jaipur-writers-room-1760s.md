# जयपुर का लेखन-कक्ष, 1760 (Jaipur Writer's Room)

## Overview

The room where Pandit Todarmalji writes the granth in 1760s Jaipur. By house rule it appears only as a hand, a white sleeve, a clay lamp and a page — never a face, never a voice. It frames the present-day story and returns as a कैरी.

## Geography

- A low चौकी holding long पन्ने; a जाली (lattice) window with moonlight.
- Timeless staging: the lamp stays fixed while the page-stack grows (समय बीत रहा है).
- Reached only by कैरी from a present-day flame, never by travel.

## Sensory Details

- Wet ink catching the lamplight; a reed pen on paper.
- No speech in the room; only दादी's narration plays over it.
- The flame fills the frame and turns blue to cross into the present (P.1).
