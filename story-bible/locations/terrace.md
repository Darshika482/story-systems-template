# छत — टेरेस (The Terrace)

## Overview

The rooftop of the building, and the संक्रांति sky full of kites. It opens the present-day story (1.2) and closes it a year later (E.1), with everyone looking up.

## Geography

- Flat roof with a मुँडेर (parapet) looking over the society and lane.
- Open to the whole kite-sky; "वो काटा!" rises from every roof.
- दादी's spot with her knitting basket and a small radio.

## Sensory Details

- Lifting winter fog at dawn turning to a bright kite-sky.
- Glass-coated kite string — the reason this family flies none.
- A cut kite drifting down into frame (1.2, E.1).
