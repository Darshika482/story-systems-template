# सोसाइटी गार्डन बेंच (Society Garden Bench)

## Overview

The सूर्योदय रेज़िडेंसी garden at dawn, with the bench दादाजी has kept for forty years. Where the household lessons continue in the open and where गुप्ता's absence is felt. See the [bench](../props/bench.md) prop.

## Geography

- Ground-level garden near the society gate and parking.
- A single two-person bench is दादाजी's fixed dawn spot.
- Adjacent to the lane and gate used in later scenes (4.4, 7.2).

## Sensory Details

- Winter fog at dawn; cold enough to borrow a shawl (2.2).
- No score under the key beat — the six-second silence when गुप्ता's death lands (1.7).
- Wet grass and birdsong by July (5.1).
