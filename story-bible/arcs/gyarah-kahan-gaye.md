# ग्यारह कहाँ गए (The Inherited Sentence)

## Overview

"ग्यारह कहाँ गए?" — [दादाजी](../characters/dadaji-hariprasad.md)'s cashier joke at अर्जुन's 89% in 1999 — is a wound passed down a generation and, at last, taken back. The arc traces the sentence from father to son to grandson and its release.

## Beats

- 1999 origin — a joke over 89%, never retracted (spoken, not shown).
- अर्जुन re-asks it of Sam's 91.4% — "आठ दशमलव छह कहाँ गए?" (3.5).
- दादाजी goes still, hearing himself; his own [card](../props/nidan-board.md) stays face down.
- Named and owned the night before surgery — "ये रोग मैंने दिया तुझे।" (6.8).
- अर्जुन signs Sam's own form without re-asking (7.2).

## Turning Points

- 3.5 — the sentence passes to the next generation.
- 6.8 — it is named and claimed by its source.
- 7.3 — released: "कहना था — आ, खाना खा ले।"
