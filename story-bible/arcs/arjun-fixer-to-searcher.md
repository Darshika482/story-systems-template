# अर्जुन — फ़िक्सर से खोजी (Arjun: Fixer to Searcher)

## Overview

[अर्जुन](../characters/arjun.md) begins as the fixer who arranges everything and cannot sleep; he ends able to look for his own error instead of a culprit. Lie: "मैं कर लूँगा — मैं कर्ता हूँ." Truth: the restlessness begins inside, and the miscount is his own.

## Beats

- Setup — P.2: the missing ₹1; no sleep.
- गुप्ता's death → a one-night vow, gone by morning (1.9–1.10).
- Result day — inherits "ग्यारह कहाँ गए?" and passes it on (3.5).
- Three days voiceless; decides to sow, not suck (4.1–4.5).
- Reads with targets (5.1); takes a spiritual shortcut, "मैं शुद्ध आत्मा हूँ" (6.4).
- Reads to his father (6.8); finds the rupee was his own (7.1).

## Turning Points

- 4.5 — decides to read: "पहले पन्ने से।"
- 6.8 — reads to दादाजी; the shortcut drops.
- 7.1 — "एक रुपया… मेरे पास।" He sleeps.
