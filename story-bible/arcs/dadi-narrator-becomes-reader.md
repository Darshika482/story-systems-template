# दादी — कहनेवाली से पढ़नेवाली (Dadi: Narrator to Reader)

## Overview

[दादी](../characters/dadi-shanti-devi.md) opens as the amused guide to everyone else's faults, forwarding each teaching to someone else and hiding that she reads slowly. She ends counting herself in and reading the granth herself.

## Beats

- Amused narrator and guide (Chapters 1–3).
- Tries to skip the dried-tulsi scene — "ये वाला ज़रूरी नहीं है।" (2.8).
- Her lips move with Sam's reading in the power cut; only मीरा sees (5.5).
- Finishes दादाजी's line from the stove — "ये बातें तुम लोगों के लिए हैं।" (6.6).
- Pins her own card on the board (7.5); reads at 2 a.m. (E.2).

## Turning Points

- 6.6 — the house learns she knows the granth by ear.
- 7.5 — she adds herself to the diagnosis; takes the glasses.
- E.2 — she reads: "मं-ग-ल-म-य।"
