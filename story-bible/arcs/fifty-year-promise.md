# पचास साल पुराना वादा (The Fifty-Year Promise)

## Overview

On the 1977 wedding night [दादाजी](../characters/dadaji-hariprasad.md) promised to teach [दादी](../characters/dadi-shanti-devi.md) to read. Fifty years of postponement end when he hands her his old [glasses](../props/dadaji-ka-chashma.md) — and it turns out she already knows the book by ear.

## Beats

- Named on the golden-jubilee stage — "पचास साल में 'अ' से 'आ' तक" (3.4).
- दादी already carries the granth by ear, hidden until 6.6.
- The promise resurfaces around the surgery; the Diwali reading opens it (7.4).
- दादाजी gives her the old thick pair — "सिर पर ही रखा रह गया।" (7.5).
- She reads at 2 a.m.; अनाया pushes the sliding glasses up (7.5, E.2).

## Turning Points

- 3.4 — the unkept promise is spoken aloud.
- 7.5 — the glasses change hands.
- E.2 — she reads: the promise is kept.
