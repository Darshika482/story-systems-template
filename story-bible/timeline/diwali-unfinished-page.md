# दिवाली — अधूरा पन्ना (Diwali, the Unfinished Page)

## Overview

Diwali. "अब साफ़ दिखता है। पढ़ेगा कौन?" Each person reads a line their own way, and the [granth](../props/granth.md)'s last page stops mid-word on "बहु" — बहुरि, "और फिर…". "है। लिखी नहीं गई।"

## When

- Friday 29 October 2027; shown in order.
- The reading-promise is kept the same night (7.5), after the surgery and new lens.

## Consequences

- The ending is unwritten by design; अनाया is made the one who will continue it.
- दादाजी hands दादी his old [glasses](../props/dadaji-ka-chashma.md) (7.5) — the [fifty-year promise](../arcs/fifty-year-promise.md) paid.
- Carries the [aur-phir](../themes/aur-phir.md) thread to its payoff.
