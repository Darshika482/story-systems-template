# मकर संक्रांति 2028 (Sankranti 2028)

## Overview

One year on, the same संक्रांति sky. अनाया lets a cut kite go; at 2 a.m. [दादी](../characters/dadi-shanti-devi.md) reads by lamplight. The story closes on "और फिर… वो तू बताएगी।"

## When

- Saturday 15 January 2028; the story's close.
- Deliberately mirrors [Sankranti 2027](sankranti-2027.md) a year earlier.

## Consequences

- अर्जुन sits on गुप्ता's half of the [bench](../props/bench.md) and lets the office ring (E.0).
- मीरा's list has a blank third line; the [cane](../props/ganne-ka-gamla.md) stands over अनाया's mark.
- The reversal of the opening is complete — reading replaces restlessness at 2 a.m.
