# रिज़ल्ट का दिन (Result Day)

## Overview

Sam's class-10 result is 91.4%. अर्जुन's first words are "आठ दशमलव छह कहाँ गए?" — and across the table दादाजी goes still, hearing his own 1999 sentence come back.

## When

- Third week of May 2027; shown in order.
- Echoes the 1999 origin ("ग्यारह कहाँ गए?") carried by [गिनती: ग्यारह कहाँ गए](../arcs/gyarah-kahan-gaye.md).

## Consequences

- Sam cuts and posts "My dad has two dialogues" (3.6); a cold war begins.
