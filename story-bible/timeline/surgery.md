# आँख का ऑपरेशन (The Surgery)

## Overview

दादाजी's cataract surgery. The night before (6.8) he turns his माला, fast and afraid; अर्जुन finds him and reads to him. In the hospital corridor दादी has promised eleven fasts.

## When

- Monday 20 September 2027; shown in order.
- After क्षमावाणी (15 Sep) and दादी's reveal (6.6); sets up the new lens in Chapter 7.

## Consequences

- The face-down [board](../props/nidan-board.md) card is turned: "ये रोग मैंने दिया तुझे।"
- अर्जुन reads to his father for the first time (6.8), a turn in his arc.
- Recovery brings the eye-shield then a new lens (7.1–7.3); the glasses can be given away (7.5).
