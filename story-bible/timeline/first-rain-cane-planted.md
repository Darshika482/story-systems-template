# पहली बारिश — गन्ना बोया (First Rain, Cane Planted)

## Overview

The discarded काना [cane node](../props/ganne-ka-gamla.md) is pressed into अनाया's old pot as the year's first rain arrives. अर्जुन, voice cracked, resolves to read — "शुरू कहाँ से?" — "पहले पन्ने से।" The film's midpoint and interval.

## When

- Late June 2027; the midpoint; shown in order.
- Follows three days of अर्जुन's voice rest (4.1–4.4); his first words return at 4.5.

## Consequences

- Turns the film from diagnosis to treatment — "अब इलाज शुरू होगा… और बीमारी कपड़े बदलेगी।"
- The cane becomes the growth/time marker through the rest of the film.
- Seeds अर्जुन's reading arc ([fixer to searcher](../arcs/arjun-fixer-to-searcher.md)).
