# गुप्ता अंकल की मृत्यु (Gupta Uncle Dies)

## Overview

[गुप्ता अंकल](../characters/gupta-uncle.md), late to the garden bench for the first time since 1987, dies suddenly at dawn. दादाजी answers the phone smiling; the smile goes. The inciting incident.

## When

- Story day 2 — Saturday 16 January 2027, dawn; shown in order.
- The morning after [Sankranti 2027](sankranti-2027.md).

## Consequences

- अर्जुन's one-night vow "अब से सब बदल दूँगा" (1.9), gone by next morning (1.10).
- अनाया's unanswered death question "गुप्ता अंकल कहाँ गए?" (1.8).
- दादी's two extra माला, "अपने वालों के लिए" (1.12); गुप्ता's half of the [bench](../props/bench.md) left empty.
