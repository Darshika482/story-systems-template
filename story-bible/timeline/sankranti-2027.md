# मकर संक्रांति 2027 (Sankranti 2027)

## Overview

Friday 15 January 2027: the संक्रांति kite-sky over Mansarovar. The present-day story opens with अर्जुन awake at 1:52 a.m., unable to account for one rupee of maintenance.

## When

- Story day 1 — Friday 15 January 2027; shown in order.
- One day before [गुप्ता अंकल dies](gupta-uncle-dies.md); mirrored a year later by [Sankranti 2028](sankranti-2028.md).

## Consequences

- Establishes the missing ₹1 (₹2,841 vs ₹2,840) that pays off in 7.1.
- Plants the चश्मा-on-the-head gag (1.2) and अनाया's claimed [cut kite](../props/kati-patang.md).
- Names the house's condition: everyone is looking up for something.
