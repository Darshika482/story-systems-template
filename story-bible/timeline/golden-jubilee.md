# स्वर्ण जयंती (Golden Jubilee)

## Overview

दादी and दादाजी's fiftieth-anniversary function, thrown on a ₹3,20,000 loan to beat Mrs Kapoor's status. मान (pride) in full dress; on stage, the unkept 1977 promise to teach दादी to read is named out loud.

## When

- Sunday 9 May 2027 (आखा तीज) — fifty years after the आखा तीज 1977 wedding.
- Guests eat before sunset (house custom); shown in order.

## Consequences

- दादी's mic line "पचास साल में 'अ' से 'आ' तक" exposes the [fifty-year promise](../arcs/fifty-year-promise.md).
- दादाजी answers about the चश्मा: "ये ज़्यादा बड़ी बात थी।"
- अर्जुन takes the London call during the garlands and misses both moments.
