# लाल ऊन (The Red Wool)

## Overview

The ball of red wool in दादी's knitting basket. दादाजी pulls from it to thread the [निदान बोर्ड](nidan-board.md), so every "case" hangs, unnoticed, from her material.

## Significance

- दादी's own thread quietly runs the diagnosis she thinks is for other people — "मेरा ऊन कहाँ जा रहा है?"
- The ball shrinks across the year, paying off when she finally pins her own card.
- A soft visual rhyme: knitting (holding a house together) becomes the lines of the illness.

## Chain of Custody

- 1.16 — दादाजी draws the first red thread from her basket for the board.
- 2.3 — मीरा pins her first card; a red thread tied from दादी's पल्लू knot.
- Through the year — the ball steadily shrinks.
- 7.5 — nearly gone as दादी pins her own '११' card.
