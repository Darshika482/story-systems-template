# ग्रन्थ — मोक्षमार्गप्रकाशक (The Granth)

## Overview

The मोक्षमार्गप्रकाशक, begun by Pandit Todarmalji in 1760s Jaipur and never finished. दादाजी reads a few pages at dawn; across the film it is handed from reader to reader.

## Significance

- The "पर्चा" for the house's one illness; reading it, not performing it, is the cure.
- Its unfinished last word "बहु" (बहुरि) = "और फिर…" — the story's refusal of an ending.
- Always on its रेहल, in a yellow cloth; never quoted beyond the quote-bank cards.

## Chain of Custody

- P.1 — written in Jaipur; left unfinished ("ग्रन्थ पूरा नहीं हुआ").
- 1.1, 1.6 — दादाजी reads it in the gold kitchen.
- 5.5 — power cut; he cannot read by lamplight and hands it to Sam.
- 6.3 — he brings it near and far and closes it: "मुझे दिखाई नहीं दे रहा।"
- 6.8 — अर्जुन reads to his father from it.
- 7.4 — the last page stops on "बहु"; "है। लिखी नहीं गई।"
