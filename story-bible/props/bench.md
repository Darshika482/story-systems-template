# बेंच — गुप्ता का आधा (The Bench)

## Overview

The dawn garden bench [दादाजी](../characters/dadaji-hariprasad.md) shared with [गुप्ता अंकल](../characters/gupta-uncle.md) since 1987. After Gupta's death his half of the seat stays pointedly empty.

## Significance

- The empty half is how the film measures the loss — no line is needed.
- It is filled only when अर्जुन sits on गुप्ता's half in a kurta and lets the office phone ring (E.0).
- The bench is where the kitchen diagnosis continues in the open air.

## Chain of Custody

- 1.7 — गुप्ता is late to the bench for the first time since 1987; he dies.
- 1.17 — अनाया sits, unknowing, on गुप्ता's half.
- 2.2 — अर्जुन joins दादाजी on the bench; borrows the rust shawl.
- E.0 — अर्जुन takes गुप्ता's half and lets the call ring twice.
