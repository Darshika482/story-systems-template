# स्टील का गिलास (The Steel Glass)

## Overview

A plain steel glass engraved on the rim ‘ह. प्र. जैन · 1977’. It is slid across the table instead of a word — water, not advice — and it bookends the film.

## Significance

- Love expressed as a gesture, not a sentence; the family's shorthand for "आ, बैठ।"
- The engraving ties it to the 1977 marriage, the same year the reading-promise was made.
- Its direction reverses at the end: son-to-father becomes grandson-to-grandmother.

## Chain of Custody

- 1.6 — दादाजी fills it and slides it to अर्जुन across the kitchen table.
- 3.1 — दादी leaves a filled glass on the parking ledge; अर्जुन drinks it warm by evening.
- E.2 — अर्जुन slides the same glass to दादी as she reads at 2 a.m.
