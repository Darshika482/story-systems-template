# कटी पतंग (The Cut Kite)

## Overview

A cut kite drifts down onto the terrace during the संक्रांति kite-sky; अनाया claims it at once — "मेरी पतंग!" The family itself flies no kite (the string is glass-coated).

## Significance

- Plants अनाया's "मेरी" — the grip of possession — on the opening day.
- Pays off a year later when she lets a cut kite go: "ये रुकने नहीं आई थी।"
- Its disappearance between is a small, early lesson in holding and losing.

## Chain of Custody

- 1.2 — a cut kite drifts down; अनाया claims it and ties it to the balcony grill.
- 2.6 — the tied kite is gone; "तेरी थी?" — "…आई तो मेरे पास थी।"
- E.1 — a cut kite lands on the terrace; अनाया holds it up and lets it go.
