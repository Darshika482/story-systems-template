# निदान बोर्ड (The Diagnosis Board)

## Overview

दादाजी's old cork board from the bank (brass corners, a yellowed bank notice on top). Each family fault gets a card; red wool threads run from every card to one covered centre card — the visual argument that all of it is one illness.

## Significance

- Makes the premise visible: six faults, one disease (उलटी समझ).
- The covered centre card is uncovered at 2.8 — मिथ्यादर्शन.
- A separate card is pinned face down: दादाजी's own case, not turned until 6.8.

## Chain of Custody

- 1.16 — board born; centre card covered with a "?"; threads from [लाल ऊन](lal-oon.md); a face-down card pinned.
- 2.8 — the centre card is uncovered: मिथ्यादर्शन = उलटी समझ.
- 6.8 — अर्जुन fetches the face-down card: "ग्यारह कहाँ गए?"
- 7.1 — अर्जुन unpins his blame-threads and brings them to the centre.
- 7.5 — दादी pins her own card — the '११' she was missing.
