# जयपुर का दीया (The Jaipur Lamp)

## Overview

The clay lamp (काजल on the spout, a steady flame) beside Pandit Todarmalji's page in the 1760s. It frames the film and glosses its title: a lamp that shows the way.

## Significance

- The opening कैरी: the lamp flame fills the frame, turns blue, becomes अर्जुन's phone glow (P.1 → P.2).
- "दीये से दीया" — one lamp lights five in the power cut, with no loss to itself (5.5).
- Returns as a कैरी to Jaipur at the Diwali reading and the closing 2 a.m. (7.4, E.2).

## Chain of Custody

- P.1 — the clay lamp on the चौकी; flame steady as the page-stack grows.
- Present day — echoed by the brass घर का दीया in दादाजी's room (1.12).
- 5.5 — storm power cut; one दीया lights five more; कैरी to the Jaipur lamp.
- 7.4, E.2 — कैरी: household flame to the Jaipur lamp; the pen laid down, the lamp burns on.
