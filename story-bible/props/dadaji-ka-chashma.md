# दादाजी का चश्मा (Dadaji's Glasses)

## Overview

दादाजी's old thick glasses, forever on his head while he searches for them. The running image of मिथ्यादर्शन, and the carrier of a reading-promise made in 1977.

## Significance

- The central picture of उलटी समझ: the thing sought is already on the head.
- The 1977 promise to teach दादी to read rides on this object; it changes hands at 7.5.
- Its state tracks the cataract plot: old pair → eye-shield → new lens → handed on.

## Chain of Custody

- 1.2 — on दादाजी's head while he hunts them; "सिर को तो पता था।"
- 3.2, 3.4 — the photo shoot and the jubilee: still "सिर पर।"
- 6.3 — he cannot read; holds the granth near, far, and closes it.
- 7.1 eye-shield → 7.2 dark glasses → 7.3+ new thin gold lens.
- 7.5 — he gives दादी the old thick pair; "सिर पर ही रखा रह गया।"
- E.1 / E.2 — on दादी's nose; अनाया pushes them up.
