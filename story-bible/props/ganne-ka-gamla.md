# गन्ने का गमला (The Cane in the Pot)

## Overview

A काना (blemished) length of sugarcane the juice vendor discards — not fit to suck, fit to sow. दादाजी plants the node in अनाया's old sunflower pot at the midpoint.

## Significance

- The midpoint turn: from consuming this life to sowing it — "बोना = … दूसरी दिशा में" (4.4).
- The plant grows each chapter and marks story time against अनाया's pencil height.
- Planted as the year's first rain falls, with अर्जुन's resolve to read (4.5).

## Chain of Custody

- 2.6 — the pot holds अनाया's sunflower (turned to face the room each morning).
- 4.4 — vendor discards the काना cane; दादाजी picks it up.
- 4.5 — the node is planted; first rain; interval.
- 5.1 sprout → 6.1 two leaves → 7.4 waist-high.
- E.1 — taller than अनाया's pencil mark.
