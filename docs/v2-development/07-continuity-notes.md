## Voice across chapters

The six chapters read as one film; vocabulary, rhythm, and each person's स्थिर tics are held
tightly. Two honest seams. First, the narrator: दादी's character-intro blocks in 1.2 run to
four-or-five clauses in one breath (e.g. the अर्जुन intro), a touch past the "three sentences"
guide in §5, though the staccato is clearly a deliberate opening device and every other V.O.
block obeys the rule. Second, 6.3 (the उत्तम-क्षमा fight) is the tonal outlier — by far the
loudest, fastest, most-overlapping scene, with six voices in rapid dashed cut-offs; it stays
technically legal (cut-offs, never simultaneous speech) but sounds like a different, hotter
register than the quiet household-task scenes around it, which is almost certainly intended as
the film's peak. Sam's Hinglish is densest in ch 1–3 and thins out by ch 6–7 as he grows into
the household Hindi — a drift that works for the arc rather than against it.
