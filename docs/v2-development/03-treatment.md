# Treatment — scene by scene

**Story year:** मकर संक्रांति, Friday 15 January 2027 → मकर संक्रांति, Saturday 15 January 2028.
**Running time:** about 108 minutes · 62 scenes · 44 reels · interval at about 60 minutes.

Card IDs (Q70, Q259…) refer to `sourcematerials/granth-quote-bank.md`. "(ours)" marks an image
that is ours, not Panditji's, and is said that way on screen.

## The spine in ten lines

1. **Prologue.** A grandmother tells a bedtime story: 260 years ago, in this city, a lamp, a hand,
   a book that was never finished. "और फिर?" The lamp becomes a phone at 1:52 a.m.
2. **Ch 1 · नींद (Jan).** A man who has everything cannot sleep. His father names the symptom. A
   friend of forty years dies; the vow it produces lasts one night.
3. **Ch 2 · मैं कौन? (Feb–Mar).** Body, room, marks, anger — each is "me" or "mine". A dead tulsi
   is watered daily. The covered card is turned: मिथ्यादर्शन, उलटी समझ.
4. **Ch 3 · चार चोर (Apr–May).** Anger in a parking slot, pride at a golden jubilee, a faked
   stomach ache, a job offer taken in a store room. Then a result, and a sentence inherited
   from 1999.
5. **Ch 4 · गन्ना (June).** Three days without a voice. A window on the world. A blemished cane
   the vendor throws away: not fit to suck, fit to sow. First rain. **Interval.**
6. **Ch 5 · उलटा नक्शा (Jul–Aug).** He starts reading — with targets. One pipe, three damp
   patches. In a power cut the lamp is passed, and the boy reads aloud.
7. **Ch 6 · भेष (Sep).** The illness changes clothes: forwards, family custom, a fast kept in
   anger, "मैं तो शुद्ध आत्मा". On the day of उत्तम क्षमा the house has its worst fight. The
   grandmother turns out to know the book by ear. The night before surgery the reader is afraid.
8. **Ch 7 · जागना (Oct).** 2 a.m. again: the missing rupee was his own. A bicycle. Five floors of
   stairs. Diwali: the last page stops on half a word.
9. **The promise.** The old glasses change hands.
10. **Epilogue (Jan 2028).** A kite is let go. At 2 a.m. the narrator reads. "और फिर…"
