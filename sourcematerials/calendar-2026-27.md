## Typical Jaipur Weather by Month (climatology; IMD Jaipur)

Source: [IMD Jaipur climate PDF](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/jaipur/docs/climate.pdf); [Climates to Travel – Jaipur](https://climatestotravel.com/climate/india/jaipur)

- **January:** Coldest month; cold mornings, morning mist/fog after western disturbances (lighter than Gangetic plain). Highs ~22°C, lows ~8°C.
- **February:** Cool, dry, pleasant; warming gradually.
- **March:** Mild → warming; comfortable, dry.
- **April:** Hot, dry; temperatures rising into the high 30s°C.
- **May:** Peak heat; dry heat, max ~40–47°C (IMD).
- **June:** Very hot pre-monsoon; relief as monsoon nears end of month.
- **July:** Monsoon; heaviest rainfall month, humid.
- **August:** Monsoon; heavy rainfall, humid (Teej/Das Lakshan season is green/wet).
- **September:** Monsoon tapering; withdraws ~mid-September; humid, warm.
- **October:** Post-monsoon transition; warm days, cooling nights, clear skies (festival season).
- **November:** Dry, cooling; pleasant days, cold nights begin.
- **December:** Cold, dry; fog/mist mornings.
- **Monsoon onset (SW monsoon) Jaipur:** climatological ~end of June to early July; monsoon season end-June → mid-September. **Actual 2026 onset date for Jaipur: UNCONFIRMED** (check IMD 2026 bulletins).
