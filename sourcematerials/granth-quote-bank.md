# Granth Quote Bank — verified lines only

Every line below was copied by the earlier writing session from the scanned Jaipur edition of
मोक्षमार्गप्रकाशक (Pandit Todarmal Smarak Trust, ed. Dr. Hukumchand Bharill) and checked against the
page (book page = PDF page − 44). Compiled here from the Draft 1 screenplay (Appendix C) and the
Movie Bible quote ledgers (beats P to 3.4).

**Rules for writers**

- Curly double quotes “ ” are reserved for Pandit Todarmalji's words. Copy them from this file
  character for character. The granth joins postpositions to nouns (दुःखका, होनेपर) — that is
  correct, never "fix" it.
- Nothing outside this file may be shown as a verbatim granth line. Where a later scene needs a
  passage that is not here, the script carries a `[[GRANTH-VERIFY …]]` note and the character
  gives only a plain-Hindi gist in their own words, never in “ ”.
- Our own analogies are introduced as ours ("मैं इसे ऐसे समझता हूँ…"), never as "पंडितजी कहते हैं".
- Panditji's analogy on pp. 50–51 uses the word "पागल". It appears only inside his quoted line;
  on screen the figure is कोई and Dadaji says "जिसकी समझ खो गई थी".
- Three OCR notes carried from the ledgers — eyeball once on the scan: p. 51 "मिथ्यादर्शन" (OCR
  read "भिथ्या…"), p. 52 "पदार्थोंका" (OCR dropped the dot), p. 53–54 page break inside
  "…साधता है, कुछ / विचार नहीं रहता।"
