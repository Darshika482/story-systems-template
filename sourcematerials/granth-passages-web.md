### Pagination caveat for the film team
The page numbers in the brief (Adhikar 3 ~pp. 55–61, ~p. 68, etc.) are the **Jaipur / Todarmal Smarak Trust Hindi edition**. The readily-searchable online copy (jainqq 006760) is the **English edition with different pagination**, so online page N ≠ Jaipur print page N. To quote the Hindi at the exact Jaipur page, use the Hindi IDs (008264/008265) or the printed book.

### Incompleteness of the granth (must-know for the film)
Confirmed by multiple secondary sources: **Moksha Marg Prakashak is unfinished (अपूर्ण).** Pandit Todarmalji died (c. VS 1825–26, in the 1760s–70s CE, as a young man) **before completing the ninth adhikar**, which is on **सम्यग्दर्शन (samyagdarshan) and its eight अंग (angas)** — the text breaks off mid-way there. Sources: "could not be completed due to the untimely passing away of the author… in spite of being incomplete it is acknowledged everywhere as a unique contribution." The **exact final printed words** of the 9th adhikar, and **Dr. Bharill's प्रस्तावना (preface)** remarks on the incompleteness/last word, **could NOT be retrieved verbatim** (WebFetch down) — flag for later confirmation.
